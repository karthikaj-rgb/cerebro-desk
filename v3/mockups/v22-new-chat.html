Why they look different today

  • Heading: New is 30px light (300) and starts lower (8vh); Today is 32px regular (400) at 5vh.
  • Layout: Today is a grid of cards with buttons and agent faces; New is one long list with grey icon tiles and chevrons, a different visual language.
  • Rows vs cards: New’s rows are 72px with 15px titles; Today’s cards are 156px with 16px titles and 20px insets.
  • Also on Today: the subtext repeats the heading (“All caught up. What’s next?” / “…What’s next?”), and “Brief for Priya” has no button, so its card looks empty.
