A · Safest

One surface

The whole home is one sentence from Cerebro. The things that need you are highlighted words; tap one and its plan opens right there. Everything else is a word in the corner or ⌘K.

  • Feels like: a calm assistant, not a dashboard
  • New: the needs live inside a sentence
  • Keeps: plans, receipts, chat as they are
Goes away: sidebar, card grid, tab bar

B · Moody

Living field

A dark canvas with one moving light. Its colour is your day: warm when something needs you, cool blue when all is calm, violet while agents work. Huge type says the one thing that matters.

  • Feels like: a car dashboard at night, or a watch face
  • New: state you notice from across the room
  • Risk: dark only; less room for detail
Goes away: lists, greys, most borders

C · Spatial

Orbit

Your AI team is a little solar system around Cerebro. Agents drift slowly; the ones that need you glow and pull their question into view. Zoom into an agent to see its work, zoom out to see the whole agency.

  • Feels like: a living map of your team
  • New: who’s busy and who’s stuck, at a glance
  • Best for: the AI team page, or a home toggle
Goes away: the agent list and grid

D · Different

Time line

There are no pages, just your day on one line. What agents finished sits to the left and fades; what needs you sits on the Now line; what’s coming waits on the right as outlines. Swipe through time instead of through menus.

  • Feels like: scrubbing through a video of your agency
  • New: past, now and next in one view
  • Risk: new idea to learn; needs good zoom
Goes away: Today, Your work and History as separate places

My pick

A · One surface as the home, with B’s light as a quiet colour wash that shows the state of your day, and C · Orbit for the AI team page. It’s the most different from every other app while still easy to use on day one. D is the boldest; worth a prototype later.
