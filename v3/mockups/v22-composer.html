Four directions, drawn from Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity / Cursor, and an agent-first one of our own. Each shown empty, while typing, and while talking. Nothing in the app is changed yet.

Agent-first our own

The box knows it’s talking to a team. “To:” picks Cerebro, an agent or a channel; a running chip shows work in progress (and lets you stop it); suggestions above change with where you are. Fits the AI team and channels we just built.

My pick: D · Agent-first, built on A’s roomy two-line box. It shows who you’re talking to and what’s running, which no general chat app needs but Cerebro does. From C, I’d take the context chips (client, memory) and maybe the Ask / Do / Plan switch later.

References: Claude (two-line box, tools below, model picker), ChatGPT (+ menu, voice mode button), Perplexity and Cursor (context chips, modes, @ mentions).
