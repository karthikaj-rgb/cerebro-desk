Campaign · less text, same information

Today every stage adds a paragraph, a steps row and three big cards, and nothing ever shrinks. Four ways to fix it. All keep the numbers (est. rate, bookings, audience size) visible.

Also a bug in your screenshot: “Picked up again” replays the whole intro, plan bar and offers a second time. Whatever option you pick, resuming will continue from where it stopped instead of starting over.
A

One living card

The whole campaign is one card that updates in place. Each stage is a row: finished rows shrink to one line with an Edit link, the current row opens to show its choice, later rows wait in grey. The chat stays short. You can always see the whole plan at once.

B

Build it on the canvas

Same pattern as the website: the chat holds one line per stage, and the campaign takes shape on the canvas as a visual brief: the offer as a hero, the audience as a number and ring, the sequence as a timeline. Choices are made in the chat; the canvas shows the result.

C

Short chat, decisions collapse

Keep the chat, but trim it hard: one short line per stage, compact choice tiles with a bar showing the expected result, and once you choose, the whole stage collapses into a chip at the top. The plan bar becomes those chips.

D

Draft first, then tweak

The most agentic: the AI drafts the whole campaign in one go and shows a single summary card (offer, audience, messages, schedule), each with its number and a Change link. One decision: schedule it. You only open what you want to change.
