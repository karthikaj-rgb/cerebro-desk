V2.1 · seven minimal directions

No gradients, no glow. Only flat colour, line, dots, type and motion. Each frame plays the whole morning on its own: hello, three things, a yes for each, all clear. Click a frame to move it along.

01

Hairline

The whole interface is one 1px line across the screen. When the AI talks, the line moves with its voice. The three things are tiny notches on it, and the words appear above the line only while they’re being said. When everything’s done, the line goes perfectly still.

02

Dot field

The screen is a quiet grid of dots. The AI isn’t a shape. It’s a place in the grid where the dots swell, and rings ripple out from it when it speaks. It drifts to whatever it’s talking about. On a yes, one ring runs out across the whole field and everything settles.

03

Weight

Typography is the data. Each thing is set at a size that matches what’s at stake: $4.8k is huge, $2.4k is smaller. The one being discussed turns bold. Once it’s done, it shrinks to a small struck-through line and the rest move up.

04

Receipt

The morning prints as a receipt. Each thing prints as a line. Your yes prints, then what was done prints with what it’s worth. At the end, a total and a tear line. Tactile, honest, and you can screenshot it.

Receipt
05

Focus

Everything is on screen at once, but out of focus, like a camera. Only what’s being said is sharp, with a thin viewfinder bracket around it. Moving to the next thing is a rack focus. No layout changes, just attention.

06

Decode

Monochrome terminal. Every line arrives scrambled and decodes itself letter by letter. The AI is a small block of characters in the corner that flickers while it talks. The three things are a checklist that ticks itself.

[ ] three accounts dipped −4.8k
[ ] ads paused since tuesday −2.4k
[ ] missing calls after 5 pm +18%
Decode
07

Hatch

Print-shop poster. The thing you’re discussing is set huge in bold type filled with moving hatch lines, like ink still drying. Say yes and it sets solid. The others wait in outline along the bottom.

My pick: 01 Hairline. It’s the most minimal thing possible (one line), it is the voice, and nothing else on the market looks like it. 05 Focus is the runner-up: same calm, and the rack focus is a beautiful way to move between things. Both work in light and dark.
