Smaller ideas that work with any of them

Time-of-day lightThe room’s colour follows the real clock: cool dawn at 7, bright at noon, warm dusk after 5.
The AI leans inWhen you hover a thing, the wave or orb drifts toward it and a faint line connects them.
Done = lightEach yes sends a small particle of light from the item into the stats line; the number ticks up.
Sound designA soft chime on done, a lower tone on later. Off by default, one toggle.
Cursor glowA faint light follows the cursor across the background, like a torch in a dark room.
Spoken captionsWords light up as they’re said (karaoke), so voice and text feel like one thing.

My pick: 01 Horizon for the look (it’s futuristic without getting in the way of the words), with The AI leans in and Done = light on top. 03 Glass stack if you want light mode to be the hero.
