Why it feels simple

The rules every screen follows.

One action per card

The most likely answer is the only button. Everything else is in the sheet.

Three tabs

Today, Talk, Chats. Team, Memory and Settings live under your avatar.

Big targets

Buttons are 44pt or more. Approving is a slide, so nothing happens by accident.

Details on demand

Two facts on screen, the rest one tap away (“See the 4 steps”).

Always undoable

Every result is a receipt with Undo, in the app and in the notification.

Same product as desktop

Same faces, cards, grey (F9F9FA) and memory. Start on the phone, finish on the laptop.
