Home and New chat, each with its own job

Home is where Cerebro comes to you: what needs you, what's running, what got done, what it suggests. New chat is where you go to Cerebro: one big box, and pickers for who it's for, what to make and what to start from. Nothing appears on both.

Home · “What's going on?”

Changes every time you open it.

  • Needs you (fixes, with money at stake)
  • Running now (live agents and progress)
  • Done while you were away (receipts, with undo)
  • Ideas from Cerebro (one or two, with evidence)
  • A small box: “Ask about today”

New chat · “What do you want to do?”

Looks the same every time.

  • One big, focused box
  • Pickers: For (a client), Make (a kind), Start from (a template)
  • Templates for the picked kind
  • Pick up where you left off (drafts, recent chats)
  • No fixes, no money, no proposals
