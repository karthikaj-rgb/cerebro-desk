Today’s page · layout options

Four directions for the home page, all on one design system, so whichever you pick, every size, gap and font is decided once and used everywhere.

What’s off in the current page (your screenshot)

  • Uneven card heights. Bento spans leave big empty areas (Onboard Glow, review replies) and a gap bottom-right.
  • Money wraps on its own line (“· −$2.4k/mo”). It should be a pill that never wraps.
  • Too many text sizes (30, 17, 15, 14, 13, 11 mono) with no clear order; the mono labels are wide and loud.
  • No next step on the card. In Flat there’s no button, so cards read as notes, not things to act on.
  • The reminder is heavier than the cards and the MRR number uses a different style from everything else.
  • No sign of the AI team: who would do the work isn’t shown.
