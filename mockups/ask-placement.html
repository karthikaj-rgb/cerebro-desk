Today the ask bar is a thin pill at the very bottom, under the cards, and it reads as a footer. The project is the AI, so the box should be the first thing the eye lands on and the thing every card points back to. Three ways to do that, from smallest change to biggest.

Option AHero bar at the bottom

Same place, but it stops being a footer. The bar spans the whole desk, is twice as tall, sits on a lilac halo, and carries three suggestion chips above it that change with the desk. The cards end above it instead of running underneath. On focus the desk dims and the conversation grows upward out of the bar.

  • Smallest change; every other layout keeps working.
  • Keeps the “answers first, question last” reading order.
  • Still at the bottom, so the eye reaches it last.

Option BCommand line on top

The box moves to the top of the desk, full width, where the Make-something row is now. It is the first thing after the greeting: you tell the desk what you want, and the cards below are what it is already doing. Make-something becomes chips inside the box’s suggestions, and the voice button sits in the bar.

  • Reads like a search or command line: “type here first”.
  • Frees the bottom, so the cards never hide behind anything.
  • Replies have to open as a room or push the cards down; there is no natural place for a thread.

Option C · recommendedThe panel is the AI

The lilac panel already is the AI: the stitched field, the creature, the greeting. So the box goes there, at the bottom of the panel, with the conversation living above it. The left side becomes “talk to the AI”, the right side “what it is doing for you”. Pressing a card’s button, circling things, or Get started all land as lines in the same thread, so the room is no longer a separate overlay for quick things; it only opens for live work.

  • The AI gets a permanent home instead of a strip.
  • Every action on the desk shows up as a line in the thread: one place to see what happened.
  • Biggest change: the room, the Get started button and the voice creature all move into the panel. Scribble and the Editorial look need their own version of it.
My pick: C, with A’s suggestion chips inside the panel
