Desk · listening, so you know it's your turn

Six states for the ask bar after the brief ends. The aim: it's obvious the mic is on, you can see what it heard while you speak, it waits for you to finish, and when it mishears it shows what it heard instead of saying “I didn't catch that”.

1 · Your turnright after the brief
Mic is on. Ask me anything, or say “fix them”.
✦Listening… go ahead
“Fix them”“What needs me?”“Build me a website”
Mic is on in words, a red recording dot, a pulsing ring on the mic and three spoken examples, so it reads as "talk now", not "type here". Plays a soft two-note chime as it opens.
2 · Hearing youlive words as you speak
Listening
✦okay that's fine, can you build me a website for…
Your words appear in the bar as you say them (grey while it's still guessing, dark once it's sure). The waveform follows your voice, so you can see it's actually hearing you.
3 · You pausedit waits instead of cutting you off
Still listening… keep going, or I'll go in a second
✦okay that's fine, can you build me a website for Glow
A short pause no longer ends your turn. A ring drains around the mic for ~1.6 s; speak again and it keeps adding to the same sentence. Tap the mic to send right away.
4 · Got itit says what it's doing
Building a website for Glow Med Spa…
✦okay that's fine, can you build me a website for Glow
Your sentence stays in the bar for a beat with a green tick, then the room opens. You always see what it understood before it acts.
5 · Not sureshows what it heard, offers the closest
I heard “fix the mall”. Did you mean:
Fix them allFix the missed callsSay it again
✦Listening… or tap one above
No dead-end “I didn't catch that”. It shows the words it got and its two best guesses; say “yes”, tap one, or just repeat. The mic stays open.
6 · Mic is offblocked, or you turned it off
Mic is off. Type below, or turn it on to talk.
✦Ask your desk anything…Turn on mic
If the browser blocked the mic, it says so plainly and gives one button. Never a silent mic that looks on.

What changes under the hood

  • Listen continuously with live results instead of one short take. Today it stops at the first pause and only returns the final guess, which is why it cuts you off and you can't see what it heard.
  • Decide you're done after ~1.6 s of silence, not the browser's default (~0.5–0.8 s). Speaking again within that window joins the same sentence.
  • Pass the browser's alternative guesses to the matcher, so “fix the mall” still lands on “fix them all”.
  • Same states in the room and on the phone (the bar there becomes option B of the phone-bar mockup).
