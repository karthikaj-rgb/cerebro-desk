Desk · the mic, while it listens

Eight ways the ask bar can show it's listening, each reacting to a simulated voice. Switch between Resting, Listening (mic on, no one talking) and Hearing you, and check light and dark.

State
✦
canvas · blob1 · Liquid mic

The button is a soft lilac blob. Listening, it wobbles gently; when you talk, it swells and ripples with your voice.

✦
canvas · ripples2 · Sonar

Thin rings leave the mic like ripples on water. Slow and faint while it waits, faster and bolder the louder you are.

✦
canvas · orbit3 · Orbit

A few dots orbit the mic. They drift slowly while listening and fling outwards when you speak.

✦
canvas · dot halo4 · Stitch halo

A ring of tiny squares from the lilac panel's grid. They light up around the mic in step with your voice.

✦
css · conic border5 · Aurora edge

The whole bar's edge becomes a slowly turning band of colour. It glows brighter and spreads as you talk. No extra shapes.

✦
canvas · line6 · Ink line

A single line in the empty part of the bar, like a pen trace of your voice. Flat when quiet, alive when you speak. Steps aside as your words fill in.

✦
dom · bars7 · Equaliser

Eight bars next to the mic, driven by your voice. The most literal, and the easiest to read at a glance.

✦
css + canvas8 · Mic becomes the orb

When the mic opens, the button turns into the room's orb at the same size, and breathes with your voice. Ties the bar to the room without crowding it.
