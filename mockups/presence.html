Desk · what the AI looks like while it talks

Six live alternatives to the orb. Each one is drawn in the browser and reacts to a simulated voice. Flip the state to see idle, listening and speaking, and the theme to check light and dark.

State
canvas · 3D points1 · Particle sphere

A globe of dots slowly turning. Listening, it breathes in; speaking, the surface ripples with your words; thinking, the dots swirl.

canvas · halo2 · Light ring

Just a thin ring of light. Its edge waves with the voice, like a sound trace bent into a circle. Very quiet when idle.

canvas · grid3 · Stitch field

The panel's dot grid, gathered into a circle. Dots light up from the centre as the voice gets louder. Ties the AI to the lilac panel.

canvas · ribbons4 · Aurora

Three soft ribbons of colour drifting across each other. They flatten to a line when idle and bloom when it speaks. Fits the Bar voice too.

canvas · rings5 · Iris

Concentric rings like a lens. They tighten when it listens to you and open out when it answers. Feels like attention.

canvas · liquid6 · Liquid drop

A soft glass drop with a moving highlight. Its outline wobbles with the voice; thinking, it slowly rotates inside itself.
